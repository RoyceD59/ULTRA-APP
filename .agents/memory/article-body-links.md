---
name: Article body links
description: Editorial requirement for how hyperlinks appear inside blog articles.
---

Article hyperlinks should be descriptive clickable linkpoints, not bare URLs or literal Markdown link syntax in the rendered article body.

**Why:** The user clarified that review pages already open correctly; the requested correction is how hyperlinks appear inside the article.

**How to apply:** Preserve this requirement when changing article generation, editorial previews or customer article readers. Use readable source names and keep unsafe targets disabled.
