description: Funding, claims and hosting scope for the Hydrate the Future forest and Companion tree.

Consumption logging is not a purchase, refill transaction, donation, tree allocation, or financial-impact event.

**Why:** The owner explicitly excluded those effects from daily hydration logging.

**How to apply:** Keep daily consumption and reminders separate from commerce, programme funding, rewards, and impact ledgers.

Hydrate the Future allocates 3% of eligible paid purchases, split equally between trees and clean-water access. Logged refills add no funding. Do not invent baseline revenue, confirmed lifetime value, donation dates, partners or field outcomes.

**Why:** The approved release scope explicitly requires those funding rules and distinguishes digital allocations from verified programme activity.

**How to apply:** Keep the card, forest and methodology consistent. Missing programme evidence or an unavailable totals API must show pending/unavailable, not substitute demonstration figures. Anonymous forest links illustrate a growth stage; they do not independently verify field status.

Programme statements must say that part of the 3% goes towards afforestation in "our community", not frame the mission as Kenya-only. Make the copy transparent and invite people to explore and join the mission.

**Why:** The owner stated the business will operate internationally and requested this wording.

**How to apply:** Use inclusive community wording in mission and allocation statements. Preserve the approved allocation rules and current eligibility limits; a copy change does not authorize changing currency handling or claiming verified work in new locations.

Preserve the supplied forest design. Cloudflare hosting is for this static forest only, not a move of the Companion, ProjectHub or API.

**Why:** The approved scope excludes rebuilding the supplied design and moving unrelated services.

**How to apply:** Use an explicit static-file allowlist and obtain separate approval for production publication, custom-domain/DNS changes and protected-branch merges.

The owner approved connecting `forest.ucfilters.com` before certification, with unverified programme information clearly marked pending.

**Why:** The owner changed the earlier certification gate and explicitly selected “Connect forest.ucfilters.com now”, approving custom-domain attachment and the required forest DNS record.

**How to apply:** Keep missing evidence null/pending; this domain approval does not authorize fabricated temporary figures, certification claims, or unrelated routing changes. Obtain documented evidence and public-disclosure approval before publishing verified outcomes.
ProjectHub is the source of truth for global UC Impact, displayed uniformly across registered accounts.

**Why:** The user asked, “Synche all registered account to display global UC Impact page to uniformly from project hub.”

**How to apply:** Read shared global figures rather than copying totals into each account. Keep global programme figures distinct from each customer's personal impact.

Companion's About Us must remain an in-app page with global impact and an external forest link, not a redirect to the forest itself.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “Create an in-app About Us page with global impact and a forest link”.

**How to apply:** Keep programme browsing available without a personal tree or customer identity; do not replace the company/app information with the external forest.
