Our growing forest

Through Hydrate the Future™, 3% of eligible Ultra Clear purchases is allocated to growing and restoring trees in Kenya. Each customer nurtures a digital tree; together they fund real ones.

Programme figures

How a tree grows

    Funded is not the same as established. A funded tree means contributions have reached its estimated cost. An established tree is one the programme has confirmed in the field. Only programme reports move trees to established.

    Methodology

    For every eligible transaction (a paid KES purchase that was not cancelled, failed or refunded):

    • customer allocation = eligible revenue × 3%
    • tree funding progress = accumulated allocation ÷ tree cost
    • estimated trees funded = total allocation ÷ tree cost

    Refills customers log in the Companion app water their digital tree but carry no revenue, so they never add funding. Links to this page carry only an anonymous tree id and a growth stage. They contain no names, contact details, amounts or percentages.
