---
name: Public SEO evidence
description: Evidence limits for public entities, Offers, article freshness and guide eligibility.
---

Unauthenticated API access alone does not prove that content is authoritative:
Companion product reads can return demo fallbacks. Structured sale claims must
come from verified published store data, never merely a successful public read.

**Why:** Demo prices and stock look like ordinary product responses. Indexing
them as real Offers would assert facts the business has not supplied.

**How to apply:** Keep the distinction between public accessibility and verified
publication when extending SEO templates or integrating a broader rendering
layer. Quote-only products have no fictional Offer. Editorial edit timestamps
are not evidence of published freshness, and guide read time is not task duration.
Human verification is required before publishing physical-location entities;
the existence of a map entry does not establish operator/address/opening facts.
