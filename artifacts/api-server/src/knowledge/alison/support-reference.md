# Ultra-Clear support and returns

## Support

For persistent problems or complex installations, customers can open a support
ticket in the app, email info@ucfilters.com, [call 0717774049](tel:+254717774049)
or [contact WhatsApp support](https://wa.me/254717774049).

## Returns

The current reference policy accepts requests within 30 days of the order date
for delivered or shipped orders. Reasons include a defective product, wrong
item, or an item not as described. Change-of-mind requests have a seven-day
window from delivery. Opened cartridges, installed/used filters and orders over
30 days are generally outside the standard eligibility rules.

Staff review each request; Alison must not promise a refund or decide eligibility.
Customers can go to Orders → their order → Request a Return. The reference
response time is two business days. Customers outside the usual window can
still contact the team for individual review.
