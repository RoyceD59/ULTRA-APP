Keep a Git-tracked development change log and a record of Git ignore rules.
Record every change needed to track progress and developmental changes.

**Why:** The user asked: “Lets keep a gitignore records,record every change we
need to track our progress and developmental changes.”

**How to apply:** Record development changes and evidence limits in tracked
project documentation. Group related edits, but do not omit their purpose,
verification or publication status. Keep private records, secrets and generated
staging ignored; do not ignore the change history itself.
