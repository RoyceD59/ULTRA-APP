---
name: Review sentiment policy
description: Privacy and interpretation boundaries for staff-only customer review analysis.
---
Preserve stars and semantic text analysis as independent assessments. A mixed
text experience is not a disagreement by itself; only opposite positive and
negative assessments count as a disagreement.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants staff to see mixed experiences and conflicts
between stars and wording, rather than letting one assessment replace the other.
The analysis is an aid to human review, not a customer decision mechanism.

**How to apply:** Do not supply stars to the text classifier or publish AI labels
on Companion reviews. Treat unavailable analysis separately from Neutral text.
Do not use sentiment to automatically change products or customer outcomes.
