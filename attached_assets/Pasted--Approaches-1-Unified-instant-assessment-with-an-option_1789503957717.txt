### Approaches

1. **Unified instant assessment with an optional physical-test stage — recommended**
   - Every completed quick check creates one server-calculated assessment and one ProjectHub water-test ticket.
   - The ticket starts as **Instant result ready** and changes to **Physical test requested** when the customer books.
   - The same summary appears on the lead/customer profile, ticket, Companion history, and Alison context.
   - Best audit trail and least duplication.

2. **Extend the existing physical-test request**
   - Add quick-result fields directly to the current booking record.
   - Faster initially, but every lead looks like a technician booking and status handling becomes confusing.

3. **Keep quick leads and physical tests separate**
   - Store the public result as a lead, then copy it into a water-test ticket when booked.
   - Clear separation, but duplicate records can drift and staff must reconcile them.

### Recommended design

#### Customer experience

- Add a public, shareable **Water Check** page and use the same flow in Companion.
- Adapt the attached six-question prototype into app components; do not embed its standalone script.
- Require first name, WhatsApp number, and explicit follow-up consent before showing results.
- Calculate the result on the server so customers cannot submit fabricated bands.
- Show an indicative profile for:
  - Hardness and scale
  - Taste and odour
  - Sediment and clarity
  - Microbial concern
- Include the location-based fluoride warning and clear language that this is not a laboratory or health assessment.
- Finish with:
  - **Book a physical water test**
  - **Send result on WhatsApp**
  - **Verify and save to my account**
- Public results attach to an existing customer only after WhatsApp/SMS verification. Before verification they remain a safely isolated lead.
- Signed-in Companion users attach automatically.

#### ProjectHub and data flow

- Every quick check creates a ProjectHub water-test ticket with the result summary, source answers, country/area, attribution, consent, lead status, and physical-test status.
- Physical booking updates the same ticket rather than creating a duplicate.
- Customer detail shows the latest quick result plus a compact history.
- Verified public results are linked to the customer account; staff can still resolve unmatched leads.
- Notification failure never loses the assessment or ticket.
- Repeated submissions and booking taps are idempotent and public endpoints are rate-limited.

#### Alison

- Update Alison’s approved water-quality guidance with the four result dimensions, recommendation rules, regional warnings, and safety disclaimers.
- Keep that general interpretation knowledge separate from customer data.
- When an authenticated customer chats, the server supplies only that customer’s latest verified water-result summary and linked ticket state.
- Do not put names, phones, addresses, or private results into the shared ProjectHub document library.
- Alison can answer questions such as “What did my quick check indicate?” and “Has my physical test been booked?” without asking the customer to repeat details.

#### Impact Card

- Replace the full-time card with a compact accessible header showing the title, headline litres, and expand/collapse chevron.
- Default to **collapsed** to reduce Account-page height.
- Expanding reveals the existing input, metrics, milestone, savings, details, and history without changing their calculations.

#### Verification

- Exact scoring tests based on the attached prototype.
- Public lead validation, consent, rate-limit, OTP, ownership, and duplicate-submission tests.
- Ticket creation and physical-booking transition tests.
- Customer-account summary and Alison cross-account privacy tests.
- ProjectHub and Companion UI/type checks plus mobile/web preview checks.
