# Kenya water-source reference

## Mains water

Nairobi mains water is treated and chlorinated, but source, storage, plumbing,
and interruptions can affect household water. Cloudiness may increase after rain.
Treatment at source does not establish safety at the customer's tap.

## Borehole water

Borehole water may contain hardness minerals, iron, fluoride, nitrates or
microbial contamination. Appearance and taste cannot establish safety.
Use professional testing to identify contaminants and appropriate treatment.
Do not assume a bottle or shower filter removes dissolved salts or fluoride.

## Surface water and mixed sources

Surface water may contain sediment, microorganisms and organic matter, especially
after rain. Estates may mix mains water and borehole backup. Ask which source is
being used rather than assuming its quality.

## Filter maintenance

Water conditions can affect cartridge life. Use the customer's in-app maintenance
schedule and published model instructions; do not guess a universal reduction
percentage or reset cleaning history.

## Water safety

An instant water check is indicative screening, not a laboratory measurement.
It cannot prove drinking water is safe. Suspected contamination, illness after
drinking, fluoride concerns and persistent problems require professional testing.
Customers can request a water quality assessment in the app.
