---
name: Swahili guide audience
description: Audience and localization scope for Companion installation guides.
---

A significant proportion of UCFilters' Nairobi customers are more comfortable reading Swahili than English. The requested localization is for self-service installation and cartridge replacement guides, not a whole-app language migration.

**Why:** The user identified reading comfort as a barrier to self-service and a source of support calls.

**How to apply:** Keep Swahili instructions usable offline and preserve technical quantities, product names, and safety warnings. Do not interpret guide localization as permission to translate unrelated app features.
