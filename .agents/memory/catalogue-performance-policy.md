---
name: Catalogue performance boundaries
description: Why public browsing acceleration must remain separate from commerce authority and release evidence.
---

Optimize public catalogue browsing separately from transaction-time price and
stock verification. A browsing snapshot is not authority for an order.

**Why:** The approved speed plan requires complete catalogue results and live
WooCommerce commerce authority; shortening a wait must not silently substitute
an old price, stock state, partial list or demonstration data on provider failure.

**How to apply:** Keep caches finite and failure-explicit, invalidate after
successful staff changes, and preserve uncached transaction reads. Local cache
benchmarks and compressed-size estimates are not deployed or physical-phone
improvement evidence; evaluate those separately with release approval.
