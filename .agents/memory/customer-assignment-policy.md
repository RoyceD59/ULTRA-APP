---
name: Customer assignment policy
description: Pre-signup reservations, ownership proof, and truthful COD source records.
---

Pre-signup customer filter assignments remain pending until a real account can
own them. Name is a display/audit field, not an ownership credential. Do not
create placeholder accounts, fake sales, or failed-registration records to
represent reservations.

**Why:** The user confirmed pre-signup assignments must survive and link after
signup, while customer contact entry alone must not authorize another person's
purchases.

**How to apply:** Keep account recovery separate from assignments. Require
trusted verified contacts or explicit authenticated staff confirmation, and
never use assignment linking to imply cash collection, settlement, rewards,
referrals, or extra verified impact. Unknown catalogue capacity and unknown
COD cash collection must remain unknown, not guessed from product names or
general order status.
